If a patient taking Apixaban requires a dental implant, it might be advisable to delay the procedure until the patient is not on the high-dose regimen. If they are on a standard dose or if the dental procedure is likely to cause bleeding, consult with the patient's prescribing clinician to assess the likely impact on bleeding risk and decide the best course of action, which may include advising the patient to miss their morning dose of Apixaban on the day of the dental treatment. It is important to follow general advice for managing bleeding risk during the procedure.
