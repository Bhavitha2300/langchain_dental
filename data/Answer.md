For a patient taking apixaban who requires a dental implant, it is advisable to consult with the patient’s prescribing clinician to assess the likely impact of the medication on bleeding risk. If the implant procedure is non-urgent, it may be possible to delay the procedure until the patient has completed their anticoagulant therapy. If the procedure is urgent, the patient may be advised to miss the morning dose of apixaban on the day of the dental treatment. It is essential to manage the bleeding risk appropriately during the implant procedure.
