Gingivitis is an early form of gum disease characterized by inflammation of the gums. Common signs and symptoms of gingivitis include:

1. **Red or Swollen Gums**: Healthy gums are typically pink and firm. In gingivitis, the gums may appear red and swollen.

2. **Bleeding Gums**: One of the most common signs. Gums may bleed during brushing, flossing, or even spontaneously.

3. **Bad Breath**: Persistent bad breath (halitosis) can be a sign of underlying gum infection.

4. **Receding Gums**: Gum tissue may start to pull away from the teeth, leading to the appearance of longer teeth.

5. **Sensitivity**: Increased sensitivity in the gums when consuming hot, cold, or sweet foods and beverages.

6. **Changes in Gum texture**: Gums may feel tender, soft, or puffy.

7. **Formation of Pockets**: Spaces may develop between the teeth and gums as the tissue pulls away.

8. **Difficulty chewing**: Discomfort or pain while biting or chewing can occur.

If you notice any of these signs, it's important to consult with a dental professional for a proper diagnosis and treatment. Early intervention can help prevent the progression to more severe forms of gum disease, such as periodontitis.
